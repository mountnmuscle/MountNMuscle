How much does hot tub removal cost in Charlotte?

A hot tub is flat-quoted from photos, because no two come out the same way. A four-person spa on a ground-level patio with a wide gate is a very different job from a seven-person tub on a second-story deck behind a fence. The quote covers everything: cutting it down if it will not fit through the gate whole, carrying it out, hauling it off and paying the disposal fee. There is no hourly clock running, and the number we confirm before we start is the number you pay.

Hot tubs are never priced at the $95 single-item rate. They are heavy, awkward and usually need cutting, so they sit in their own category alongside pianos, gun safes and slate pool tables. To get a real number fast, text 704-237-0164 with:

  • A photo of the tub and one of the path from the tub to the driveway or street
  • Where it sits: patio, deck, sunroom or set into the ground
  • Whether the gate or walkway is narrower than the tub
  • Any steps between the tub and the truck
  • Whether the cover, steps or surround go too

What do I need to do before the crew arrives?

Two things, and both matter. First, shut the power off and have it disconnected at the breaker or the outdoor disconnect. Hot tubs run on a dedicated high-amp circuit, and that disconnect is electrical work: we do not open panels, and we do not cap or reconnect wiring. If you are not sure the circuit is dead, have an electrician make it safe before the appointment.

Second, drain it. A full tub holds hundreds of gallons, and a few inches of standing water turns a two-hour job into a mess. Most tubs have a drain spigot near the base; a garden hose on it, run to a spot that drains away from the house, empties it over a day or two. Pull the filters and any loose chemicals out of the cabinet before we arrive, since pool chemicals cannot ride in the trailer with everything else.

Can you remove a hot tub from a deck or a backyard with a narrow gate?

Yes. That is the most common version of this job. When a tub will not fit through the gate or down the deck stairs in one piece, we cut it into sections on site and carry the pieces out, so nothing gets dragged across the lawn and no fence panels come down. Tell us about the stairs and the gate in the photos and it is already built into the quote.

In-ground and sunken tubs, and tubs boxed into a deck or a stone surround, are quoted after a closer look, because removing the surround is part of the work. If you want the empty pad or the deck section where the tub sat cleaned up afterward, ask, and that goes in the same quote.

What happens to the old hot tub?

The shell, cabinet and cover go to a licensed disposal site, and the metal parts get pulled out for recycling first. Steps and covers that are still in good shape go to donation when someone will take them. Everything leaves on the same visit, and we sweep up the spot before we go.

Clearing out more than the tub? Patio furniture, an old grill, a playset or a shed can ride on the same visit. See the junk removal price ladder for everything else, or our pages on appliance removal and mattress removal.
