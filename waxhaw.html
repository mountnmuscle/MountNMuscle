Loads are priced by how much of the trailer you actually fill: a 5x8 utility trailer is $145 quarter, $210 half, $285 packed, and a 6x10 utility is $170 quarter, $230 half, $335 packed. For barn, basement and whole-estate cleanouts we bring the 16ft enclosed trailer, priced at $150 for 1/8, $235 for 1/4, $315 for 3/8, $395 for 1/2, $475 for 5/8, $560 for 3/4 and $700 for a full load. Surcharges are flat and stated up front: mattress or box spring +$25 each, refrigerant appliance +$40, tire +$8, and paint or e-waste billed at whatever the disposal facility charges. Stairs are +$25 per flight above the first, a heavy or awkward single item starts at +$40, and same-day service is +$40.

Waxhaw generates bigger loads than most of the towns around Charlotte, and the reason is square footage plus outbuildings. A house in Millbridge or Lawson has a three-car garage and a walk-up bonus room. A place off Waxhaw Creek Road or Bonds Grove Church Road has a pole barn, a tack room, a run-in shed and thirty years of fencing, feed sacks and hay-loft furniture. The 6x10 handles most garage and single-room jobs; anything involving an outbuilding usually needs the enclosed trailer.

What will Waxhaw's town bulk pickup take, and what won't it?

Waste Pro handles bulk collection for Town of Waxhaw residents on Fridays, and you have to call and schedule it before anything goes to the curb. They will take furniture, mattresses, box springs, appliances, televisions and general household goods. They will not take building and renovation materials, and that is where most people get stuck.

Yard waste has its own limits: reusable containers or paper bags only, 30 gallons and 25 pounds maximum, up to 15 bags a week per household, plus up to three bundles of limbs cut to 3-foot lengths and no more than 4 inches in diameter. A tree that came down across a pasture fence is not a bagged-yard-waste problem, and a bathroom gut is not a bulk-pickup problem.

If you haul it yourself, the Union County landfill on Austin Chaney Road in Wingate takes furniture, mattresses, construction debris, appliances, scrap metal, e-waste and tires off the rim, at $20 per passenger-vehicle load, Monday through Friday 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to noon. Unsecured loads are charged double, so tarp and strap before you leave the driveway. The county's residential waste and recycling centers in Monroe and Wingate are open Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays only, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., and they are built for bagged household garbage and recycling, not a trailer of furniture. From most Waxhaw addresses that landfill run is 30-plus miles east and most of an afternoon by the time you load, drive, unload and drive back.

Does my Waxhaw address actually get town bulk pickup?

Only if you are inside Waxhaw town limits, and a 28173 mailing address is not the same thing. A large share of what people call Waxhaw is unincorporated Union County, or is legally in the towns of Marvin, Weddington or Mineral Springs, and those addresses get no Waxhaw municipal bulk collection at all.

In practice this splits along the roads. The Providence Road (NC-16) corridor and the Marvin Road side of things, where you find Providence Downs South, Skyecroft, Marvin Creek and the Longview area, frequently carries a Waxhaw mailing address on a non-Waxhaw parcel. The same is true going north toward Weddington. Meanwhile the New Town Road and Cuthbertson Road corridor, the Waxhaw-Indian Trail Road side, and the neighborhoods ringing the historic core are more likely to be in town.

If you are not sure, the fast test is whether the town's hauler picks up your regular trash. If nobody municipal is coming, private hauling is the only option short of driving it to Wingate yourself, and that is most of what we do out here.

What does TV mounting cost in Waxhaw?

TV mounting is $99 up to 60 inches, $149 for 61-84 inches, $239 for 85-95 inches, and 96 inches and up starts at $349 with a two-person minimum. Mounting over a fireplace or onto stone or brick starts at $199, which covers a lot of this town, because the great rooms in the estate subdivisions off Providence Road and out toward Longview and Providence Downs are built around floor-to-ceiling stacked stone.

Add-ons are never discounted: stick-on cord concealment $39, in-wall cord concealment from $89, flat mount $40, full-motion mount $65. Pricing assumes you already have a bracket unless you buy a mount from us. A second or third TV on the same visit gets 25% off the mount labor, which is how most of the newer builds in Millbridge, Cureton and Lawson go: living room, bonus room, primary bedroom, sometimes a covered porch.

Two Waxhaw-specific wrinkles. Down in the old part of town near Main Street and the tracks, you get plaster over lath, balloon framing and studs that are not on 16-inch centers, so the anchor plan gets decided at the wall, not on the phone. Out on the farms, people want a screen in a barn aisle, a shop or an outdoor kitchen, and those need a full-motion mount and a real conversation about where the cable is coming from before anything gets drilled.

Can you help with moving, loading and general labor in Waxhaw?

Add a utility trailer for +$35/hr or the 16ft enclosed for +$60/hr, so a two-person crew with the enclosed trailer is $195/hr. On Saturday and Sunday the two-person hourly crew carries a 3-hour minimum; solo labor stays at 2 hours. The Sunday +15% applies only to flat-rate work like TV mounting and junk removal, never to hourly crew work.

Most Waxhaw labor calls are load-and-unload help, in-home moves, furniture that needs to go up to a bonus room, appliance swaps, playset and trampoline teardowns, and getting a rented truck packed properly. Stairs are +$25 per flight above the first and a heavy or awkward item starts at +$40, which is what a gun safe, a slate pool table or a chest freezer coming out of a basement usually falls under.

Access is the thing to plan for here. Long gravel and dirt drives on the west side mean we stage closer to the door with a trailer than a box truck could manage, but soft ground after rain changes where we can park. Gated neighborhoods with guard gates need us on the visitor list before the appointment, and several HOAs restrict how long a trailer or a pile can sit visible at the curb, so we load out the same visit rather than staging overnight. Downtown, the block grid, the freight line through the middle of town and weekend event parking on Main make a small trailer the right tool.

Do you install flooring and do handyman work in Waxhaw homes?

LVP and laminate installation is $2.50/sq ft, herringbone and premium patterns are $3.50/sq ft, tear-out and haul-away is $1.00/sq ft, and subfloor prep is $70/hr. The minimum is 200 sq ft or $500. Waxhaw is Zone 3, so the $49 trip fee applies to flooring jobs as well.

The bulk of the flooring work in this town is builder-grade carpet coming out of houses built between roughly 2001 and 2019 in the big subdivisions, where the original carpet on stairs, in bonus rooms and in bedrooms has hit the end of its life at the same time across a whole street. Those are clean, square, predictable jobs, and the tear-out and haul line matters because the town's curbside bulk service will not take carpet and pad.

The older houses near Main Street, Broome Street and Church Street are the opposite. Those have settled, and the subfloor under the old flooring is usually the real job, which is what the $70/hr subfloor prep line is for. On the handyman side, the recurring Waxhaw list is barn and shed repairs, gate and fence hardware, shelving and garage storage in three-car garages, door adjustments in houses that have moved, mailbox and light-post work along long drives, and mounting and anchoring after a move.
