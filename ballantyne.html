Can I just put bulky junk at the curb in Ballantyne?

Sometimes, but only if your address gets City of Charlotte garbage service, and only by appointment. Charlotte bulky item collection is scheduled ahead through CharMeck 311 (704-336-7600), the CLT+ app or the city service request site, and items go to the curb no earlier than the day before and no later than 6 a.m. on the confirmed day.

The city takes furniture, mattresses and box springs, appliances with doors removed and emptied, rolled carpet and padding, lawn equipment with fuel and batteries pulled, and lumber with the nails out. It does not take construction and demolition debris, drywall, siding, roofing, concrete, brick, hazardous materials, pressurized tanks or yard waste on that same run. That exclusion is the one that catches Ballantyne homeowners most often, because a bathroom demo, a deck teardown or a flooring tear-out in a Bridgehampton or Ballantyne Meadows house produces exactly the material the city will leave sitting at the curb.

The second catch is that plenty of Ballantyne addresses are not on city collection at all. Apartments, most of the townhome communities and several of the newer attached-product neighborhoods use a private hauler with compactors and dumpsters, and those contracts generally do not include bulky pickup for a resident. If you live behind a gate or in a townhome row, check with the management office before you assume a sofa can sit out. Many Ballantyne HOAs also have covenants about how long anything can sit visible at the curb or in a driveway, and some hold architectural review authority over dumpsters, PODS and visible storage, which is why a same-visit haul-off is usually the cleaner path here than a staged pile.

Where can Ballantyne residents haul junk themselves?

The closest Mecklenburg County full-service center to Ballantyne is the Foxhole Disposal and Recycling Center at 17131 Lancaster Highway, Charlotte NC 28277 — the same zip code as most of Ballantyne. The recycling side is open Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., and the landfill side is Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m.

County residents need a free vehicle sticker to use it. Recyclables, scrap metal appliances and household hazardous waste are free for residents, along with up to five tires off the rim. A single bulky item is free; additional bulky items are charged per unit, and construction and demolition material is charged by the ton with a load minimum. Yard waste is separate again. If you are only getting rid of one dresser and you own a truck, Foxhole is genuinely the cheapest option in south Charlotte and we will tell you so.

What sends most Ballantyne calls our way instead is volume and access. A relocation cleanout is four or five trips in a Tahoe, the SC line is a few minutes south so trips to Indian Land or Fort Mill do not help with a Mecklenburg facility sticker, and Johnston Road and Rea Road at 5 p.m. turn a 20-minute round trip into an hour. Note that refrigerant appliances and mattresses have their own handling either way, which is where our +$40 and +$25 surcharges come from — that is disposal cost passed through, not a markup.

How much does TV mounting cost in Ballantyne, including over a stone fireplace?

TV mounting starts at $99 for sets up to 60 inches, $149 for 61 to 75 inches, $199 for 76 to 85 inches, and from $299 for 86 inches and up, which is a two-person minimum job. Mounting over a fireplace or into stone or brick starts at $199, and that is the request we get most in Ballantyne.

The reason is the housing stock. The 2000s and 2010s builder product across Ballantyne West, Thornhill, Provincetowne and Elon Park put a big stacked-stone or brick fireplace in the great room, and the 2020s answer to that is an 85-inch panel above it. Stone and brick need masonry anchors, not drywall toggles, and the box and outlet almost never line up with where the TV actually wants to sit. Bonus rooms over three-car garages are the second most common spot, and the media rooms in the larger Ballantyne Country Club and Piper Glen homes are the third.

Add-ons are priced separately and are never discounted: stick-on cord concealment $39, in-wall cord concealment from $89, flat mount $40, full-motion mount $65. Pricing assumes you already have a bracket unless you buy a mount from us. If you are hanging more than one TV on the same visit — a common ask when a corporate transfer family unpacks and wants the great room, the primary bedroom and the bonus room done at once — each additional TV is 25% off the mount labor.

Do you do moving labor, loading and garage cleanouts in Ballantyne?

Yes. Labor is $70/hr for a solo handyman and $135/hr for a two-person crew, both with a 2-hour minimum, billed in 30-minute increments after that. Add a utility trailer for +$25/hr or the 16ft enclosed for +$60/hr, so a two-person crew with the enclosed trailer is $195/hr.

Ballantyne generates a specific kind of labor work because of Ballantyne Corporate Park and the Class A office space around it. Corporate transfers move in and out of this area constantly, which means load-only and unload-only help, container and rental truck loading, storage runs, and the awkward middle jobs full moving companies do not want: the gun safe going to the basement, the treadmill coming out of the bonus room, the eight-foot dining table that has to come apart to clear a staircase turn, the peloton nobody has touched since the last move.

The other steady driver is the three-car garage. Ballantyne has thousands of them and they fill with holiday tubs, old sports equipment, leftover flooring, exterior paint and the furniture from the previous house. Two people and the enclosed trailer will empty and sort one in a half day, and anything worth donating goes to a donation drop rather than a landfill when it is in shape for it. Weekends run a little differently: on Saturday and Sunday the two-person hourly crew has a 3-hour minimum, the solo handyman stays at 2 hours, flat-rate work like TV mounting and junk removal has no minimum at all, and the Sunday +15% applies only to flat-rate work, never to hourly crew time.

What do flooring and handyman jobs cost in Ballantyne?

LVP and laminate installation is $2.50 per square foot, herringbone and premium layouts are $3.50 per square foot, tear-out and haul-away of the old floor is $0.50 per square foot, and subfloor prep is $70/hr. There is a minimum of 200 square feet or $500.

The typical Ballantyne flooring job is replacing builder-grade carpet on a main level or in a bonus room, often right before or right after a relocation closes. The tear-out line matters here more than in older parts of Charlotte, because carpet, pad and tack strip out of a 2,800 square foot main level is real volume and Charlotte curbside will take rolled carpet on a scheduled bulky run but will not take it mixed in with demo debris. Bundling the tear-out with the install means one crew, one trailer and one trip.

General handyman work runs at the same $70/hr solo rate with the 2-hour minimum: shelving and garage organization, closet systems, curtain rods and blinds across a wall of two-story windows, furniture assembly, door and hardware adjustments, mirrors and heavy art on stone. Card payments carry a 3.5% processing fee; cash, check and Zelle do not.
