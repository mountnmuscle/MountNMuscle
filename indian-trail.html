How much does junk removal cost in Indian Trail, NC?

A single item is $95, and trailer loads run from $145 for a quarter of a 5x8 utility trailer up to $700 for a full 16ft enclosed trailer. Indian Trail is Zone 3, so add the $49 trip fee to whichever load size you land on.

The 5x8 utility trailer prices at $145 quarter, $210 half and $285 packed. The 6x10 utility is $170 quarter, $230 half and $335 packed. The 16ft enclosed steps up in eighths: $150 for 1/8, $235 for 1/4, $315 for 3/8, $395 for 1/2, $475 for 5/8, $560 for 3/4 and $700 full. Most Brandon Oaks or Fieldstone Farm garage cleanouts land somewhere between a packed 6x10 and half of the enclosed once the old shelving, bikes the kids outgrew and the treadmill all go in together.

Surcharges are added on top and are not negotiable because they are pass-through disposal costs: $25 per mattress or box spring, $40 per refrigerant appliance, $8 per tire, and paint or e-waste at whatever the disposal site charges. Stairs above the first flight are +$25 per flight, heavy or awkward single items start at +$40, and same-day service is +$40.

  • 6x10 utility trailer: $170 quarter / $230 half / $335 packed

What will Indian Trail bulk pickup not take, and where else can I dump it?

The town's curbside bulk and e-waste service runs four times a year on your regular recycling day, and it flatly will not take contractor or renovation remnants, drywall, concrete, brick, ceramic tile, roofing materials, central air and heating units, tires, auto parts, paint, hazardous chemicals, wooden pallets or fire-damaged items. Those are the loads that end up on a trailer.

What it does take comes with strict conditions most people find out about the morning the truck skips their pile. Appliances have to be empty, refrigerators and freezers need the doors removed, only window AC units qualify, beds have to be dismantled, dressers and hutches have to be emptied with any glass taped, toilets have to be split into tank and bowl, plywood and paneling can be no larger than one inch thick by four feet by four feet, gutters have to be four feet or less, wood fencing needs the nails flattened and has to stay under 75 pounds, and carpet has to be rolled and cut to four feet or shorter with a four-roll limit at 25 pounds each. Set everything out by 7 a.m. and at least three feet clear of your carts.

That last rule is the one that catches Indian Trail homeowners mid-project. If you tore out the carpet in a bonus room, the town wants it cut into short rolls under 25 pounds. If you replaced a builder-grade vanity, that is a renovation remnant and it is not going anywhere on a town truck.

If you would rather haul it yourself, the Union County Landfill at 2125 Austin Chaney Road in Wingate is open Monday through Friday 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to noon. Residential tipping runs $20 for a car, van, SUV or pickup, $20 for a single-axle trailer loaded no more than two feet high, $40 for a single-axle trailer loaded higher, and $80 for a dual-axle trailer or a covered or box truck. From Indian Trail that is a real drive out US-74 through Monroe and past Wingate, and unsecured loads are charged double, so if you are borrowing a neighbor's open trailer in Crismark, bring a tarp and straps.

The county's five convenience centers - Parkwood, Piedmont on Love Mill Road, Goldmine Road, Armfield and White Store - are open Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays only, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. They handle bagged household garbage under the Trash Pass program, free recycling, used motor oil up to five gallons and e-waste at every site except White Store. They are not set up for a garage's worth of furniture. If your load is bigger than what fits in the back of an SUV, you are looking at the landfill or a hauler.

  • Town bulk and e-waste: 4 times per year, on your recycling day, out by 7 a.m.
  • Never accepted curbside: drywall, concrete, brick, tile, roofing, tires, paint, pallets
  • Union County Landfill, Wingate: Mon-Fri 7-4, Sat 8-12, $20-$80 residential
  • Convenience centers: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday only, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

What does TV mounting cost in an Indian Trail house?

TV mounting is $99 up to 60 inches, $149 for 61-75 inches, $199 for 76-85 inches, and a custom quote for 86 inches and up with a two-person minimum. Mounting over a fireplace or onto stone or brick starts at $199. Zone 3's $49 trip fee applies.

Indian Trail's 2000s and 2010s subdivisions were built with a specific set of walls that make this job what it is. The great-room stacked-stone fireplace with a recessed niche above it. The bonus room over the garage with a knee wall and no blocking behind the drywall. The covered back patio a lot of Bonterra and Taylor Glenn owners have since screened in. Those all take longer than a plain drywall-and-stud mount, which is why fireplace, stone and brick work starts higher.

Add-ons are priced separately and never discounted: stick-on cord concealment $39, in-wall cord routing from $89, a flat mount $40, a full-motion mount $65. Prices assume you already have your own bracket unless you buy a mount from us. If you are hanging more than one TV on the same visit - common in a house with a great room, a bonus room and a primary bedroom - each additional TV gets 25% off the mount labor.

Can I hire two guys and a trailer in Indian Trail for a few hours?

Yes. A two-person crew is $135/hr with a two-hour minimum, and a solo handyman is $70/hr with a two-hour minimum. Add a utility trailer for +$35/hr or the 16ft enclosed for +$60/hr, which puts a two-person crew with the enclosed trailer at $195/hr. Time is billed in 30-minute increments after the minimum.

This is the format that fits most Indian Trail work. Households here skew toward young families in three- and four-bedroom houses with two-car garages, and the jobs are load-a-POD-in-the-driveway, shuffle-the-bonus-room-furniture, get-the-sectional-up-the-stairs, clear-the-garage-before-the-HOA-inspection. You are not paying for a full moving company; you are paying for hours and muscle.

On Saturday and Sunday the two-person hourly crew carries a three-hour minimum. The solo handyman stays at two hours. Flat-rate work - TV mounting and junk removal - has no minimum at all on weekends. Sunday adds 15% to flat-rate work only; hourly crew rates never get the Sunday uplift. Many Indian Trail households are dual-commuter into Uptown or the US-74 corridor, so weekend windows book up first.

  • 16ft enclosed trailer add-on: +$60/hr

How much is new flooring in an Indian Trail home?

LVP and laminate install is $2.50/sq ft, herringbone and premium patterns are $3.50/sq ft, tear-out and haul-away of the old floor is $1.00/sq ft, and subfloor prep is $70/hr. There is a minimum of 200 sq ft or $500, whichever is greater, plus the $49 trip fee.

The common Indian Trail job is pulling builder-grade carpet out of a downstairs living area or a bonus room over the garage and putting LVP down. A lot of the housing stock here went up between roughly 2003 and 2020, which means the original carpet is now at the end of its life all at once across whole subdivisions. Tear-out at $1.00/sq ft matters here because that ripped carpet and pad is exactly what the town's four-times-a-year bulk service will refuse unless it is cut into four-foot rolls under 25 pounds - we just take it.

Check your covenants before you schedule. Many Indian Trail HOAs restrict dumpsters, PODs and contractor trailers parked on the street or in a driveway past a set number of days, and some require advance notice. A same-day haul-out sidesteps most of that entirely.

Do you charge extra to come out to Indian Trail?

Yes - Indian Trail is Zone 3, which carries a $49 trip fee on every job. It is stated up front, it does not change based on how far into town you are, and it is the same whether you are off Chestnut Square, near Crossing Paths Park, or out past the Sun Valley schools.

We are owner-operated out of Steele Creek in southwest Charlotte, which means the run to Indian Trail is I-485 around the bottom of the county or straight out US-74 through Matthews. That is a real 35 to 50 minutes depending on the hour, and the $49 covers it rather than getting buried in an inflated hourly rate.

Payment is cash, check, Zelle or card. Card payments carry a 3.5% processing fee. Same-day service is often available and adds $40 when it is. Our Friends & Neighbors referral program gives the referrer a $25 credit and takes $25 off the new customer's first booking; three booked referrals earns either a $100 credit or a free hour of solo labor - which tends to move quickly in a town where whole cul-de-sacs are on the same neighborhood app.
