How much does mattress removal cost in Charlotte?

Prices updated November 2026

One mattress is $120. That is the $95 single-item pickup plus a $25 fee per mattress or box spring. Every mattress and every box spring in a load carries that same +$25, so a bed set with a mattress and a box spring counts as two pieces. Bed frames, headboards and the rest of the bedroom ride along on the regular price ladder, priced by the space the load takes.

  • One mattress: $120 ($95 pickup + $25 mattress fee)
  • Each additional mattress or box spring: +$25
  • Stairs: +$25 per flight above the first
  • Same-day pickup: +$40
  • Bigger loads: the junk removal price ladder, from $150 for a pickup-bed load

Travel is built into the number we quote you, and the price is confirmed before anything gets loaded. Cash, check and Zelle carry no fee; cards add 3.5%.

Do you carry the mattress out of the bedroom?

Yes. You do not need to drag it to the curb or wrestle it down the stairs. We pick it up from the bedroom, the garage, the guest room or the curb, and the only access charge is +$25 per flight of stairs above the first. Apartments and townhomes are routine; tell us the floor and whether there is an elevator when you text.

If the mattress is going out because a new one is coming in, we can take the old frame apart as part of the same visit. We disconnect and take things apart to haul them, but we do not reassemble or install the new bed.

Why not just set it out for bulk pickup?

You can, where your town offers it, but bulk pickup usually means waiting for the collection week, following the prep rules and leaving a mattress at the curb in the rain. Some apartment communities do not allow mattresses at the trash compactor at all. A booked pickup gets it gone on the day you pick, without a fine from the leasing office or a soggy mattress in the driveway.

What happens to the mattress?

Mattresses go to a disposal or recycling site rather than an illegal dump, and the steel springs get recycled where the site takes them. We never leave anything at the curb.
