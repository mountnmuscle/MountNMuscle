How much does junk removal cost in Matthews, NC?

A single item is $115, and loads are priced by how much of a trailer they fill: a 5x8 utility trailer runs $145 quarter, $210 half, $285 packed; a 6x10 utility runs $170 quarter, $230 half, $335 packed. For a whole-house cleanout we bring the 16ft enclosed trailer, priced in eighths from $150 for 1/8 up to $700 full, with 1/4 at $235, 3/8 at $315, 1/2 at $395, 5/8 at $475 and 3/4 at $560. Matthews is Zone 3, so add the $49 trip fee.

A few items carry their own charge because the disposal site charges us: $25 per mattress or box spring, $40 for any appliance holding refrigerant (fridge, freezer, window or through-wall AC, some dehumidifiers), $8 per tire, and paint or e-waste at whatever the facility charges. Stairs above the first flight add $25 per flight, an unusually heavy or awkward item starts at +$40, and same-day service is +$40 when we can fit it. We're often available same day in Matthews, but we don't promise a window we can't hold.

Most Matthews calls land in the 5x8 or 6x10 range: a garage that finally got emptied out, a basement or crawlspace storage area, a bedroom set after a parent moved into assisted living, or the pile that builds up along the side of the house in a neighborhood where the HOA doesn't want it sitting there.

What won't Matthews bulk pickup take, and where does that stuff actually go?

The town's contracted hauler charges roughly $25 for one bulky item (up to about the size of an 84-inch couch), $50 for two to five items, and $25 per appliance — but it will not take construction and demolition debris at all. That's the gap we fill. Torn-out carpet, old deck boards, cabinets, drywall, fencing, a demoed vanity, shed contents, and anything past the five-item cap are all on you to move, and none of it goes in the green cart.

Yard waste has its own limits: paper lawn and leaf bags only, capped at 20 bags a week at 40 pounds each, and limbs cut to 4-foot lengths, no more than 5 inches thick, stacked in no more than three piles. A single storm cleanup in an older Matthews yard with mature oaks blows past that in one afternoon.

If you'd rather do it yourself, the two closest Mecklenburg County full-service disposal and recycling centers are Hickory Grove at 8007 Pence Road and Foxhole at 17131 Lancaster Highway, both open Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. Either one is a 25 to 35 minute run from most Matthews addresses depending on traffic on US-74 and I-485. Bring a truck and someone to help you unload. Also worth knowing: televisions and computer equipment have been banned from North Carolina landfills since July 1, 2011, so an old rear-projection TV can't just be set at the curb — it has to be routed to an e-waste stream, which is what the disposal-cost pass-through on our end covers.

What does TV mounting cost in Matthews?

A standard wall mount is $99 up to 60 inches, $149 for 61-84 inches, $239 for 85-95 inches, and from $349 for 96 inches and up, which is a two-person job. Mounting over a fireplace or into stone or brick starts at $199. A second TV on the same visit takes 25% off the mount labor. Prices assume you already have a bracket; if you want us to supply one, a flat mount is $40 and a full-motion is $65.

Cord concealment is separate and never discounted: $39 for the stick-on raceway channel, or from $89 for in-wall routing. In-wall is the one that depends most on the house. A lot of Matthews homes built in the 1990s and 2000s have standard drywall over wood studs and drop a cord easily. Homes near downtown and in Crestdale are a different story — older framing, plaster in places, brick chimney chases, and fireboxes with a mantel and stone surround where the only real option is a raceway or running the cord to the side. We'll tell you which one you're dealing with before we drill anything.

Two Matthews-specific notes: many living rooms in the newer subdivisions have that two-story great room with the TV niche above the fireplace, which usually needs a full-motion mount to get the screen angled down, and the Zone 3 $49 trip fee applies to mounting visits the same as anything else.

Do you do moving help and hourly labor in Matthews?

Yes — a solo handyman is $70/hr with a 2-hour minimum, and a two-person crew is $135/hr with a 2-hour minimum. Add a trailer if you need capacity: +$35/hr for a utility trailer or +$60/hr for the 16ft enclosed, so a two-person crew with the enclosed trailer is $195/hr. After the minimum we bill in 30-minute increments, not rounded up to the hour.

On Saturday and Sunday the two-person hourly crew carries a 3-hour minimum; the solo handyman stays at 2 hours. Flat-rate work like TV mounting and junk removal has no minimum at all. Sunday adds 15% to flat-rate work only — it never touches hourly crew rates.

The hourly work in Matthews tends to be load-and-unload help for a POD or rental truck, moving a household within town (a lot of that here — people downsizing out of a four-bedroom in an established subdivision into a villa or townhome without leaving the 28105 zip), shuffling a garage or attic so the floor is usable again, or getting a gun safe, treadmill, sleeper sofa or upright piano through a doorway. If you're working downtown near Trade Street on a Saturday morning, tell us — the farmers market on North Trade brings real traffic and parking is tight, and we'd rather schedule around it than sit in it on your clock.

Which Matthews neighborhoods do you work in, and what kind of work comes out of them?

We cover all of Matthews and the streets that bleed into Mint Hill, Stallings and Indian Trail — including Sardis Forest, Sardis Plantation, Brightmoor, Callonwood, Shannamara, Matthews Plantation, Weddington Ridge, Providence Hills, Chestnut Oaks, Fairfield Plantation, Deerfield Creek, Matthews Grove, Crestdale and the older blocks around downtown. Most of Matthews' housing stock went up between the early 1980s and the mid-2000s, and that age drives the work.

In the 1980s and 1990s subdivisions off Sardis Road and Sam Newell Road, we see original owners downsizing after 30 years in the same house: a full garage, an attic nobody has opened since the kids left, a den full of furniture that isn't going to the next place. In the 2000s-era neighborhoods south toward Weddington Road and McKee Road, it's more often carpet coming out for LVP, a playset or trampoline being cut down, and the appliance swap that leaves a dead fridge in the garage. Around downtown Matthews and Crestdale the houses are older and smaller, with crawlspaces, detached garages, narrow driveways and mature tree cover — worth mentioning when you call so we bring the right trailer instead of one we can't turn around.

HOAs matter here. Plenty of Matthews associations don't want furniture or bagged debris sitting at the curb for days waiting on a scheduled pickup, so people book a haul that clears it the same visit. If your community has a gate code, a specific service entrance, or a rule about trailers parking overnight, tell us up front.

Do you install flooring and do handyman work in Matthews homes?

Yes. LVP and laminate install is $2.50/sq ft, herringbone or premium patterns are $3.50/sq ft, tear-out and haul-away of the old floor is $1.00/sq ft, and subfloor prep is $70/hr. There's a minimum of 200 sq ft or $500, whichever is greater, plus the $49 Matthews trip fee.

The common Matthews job is pulling 20-plus-year-old carpet out of a downstairs and putting LVP down through the entry, dining room, family room and hallway. Because we haul, the carpet, pad and tack strip leave with us instead of piling up beside the driveway — that's the $1.00/sq ft tear-out line, and it's the part the town's bulk service won't collect. Homes with slab-on-grade and homes with crawlspaces behave differently underfoot, and older houses near downtown often need subfloor prep before anything goes down; we'll flag it rather than float a floor over a problem.

Handyman work runs at the same $70/hr solo rate with a 2-hour minimum: shelving, closet systems, curtain rods, doorknobs and locks, toilet swaps, dryer vents, storm doors, furniture assembly, picture walls, drywall patching. Payment is cash, check or Zelle at no extra cost; card payments carry a 3.5% processing fee.
