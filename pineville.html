How much does junk removal cost in Pineville, NC?

A single item is $95 anywhere in Pineville. Past one item we price by the space your load takes up: a 5x8 utility trailer is $145 quarter-full, $210 half, $285 packed. A 6x10 utility trailer is $170 quarter, $230 half, $335 packed. The 16ft enclosed trailer runs $150 at one-eighth, $235 at a quarter, $315 at three-eighths, $395 at half, $475 at five-eighths, $560 at three-quarters and $700 full.

Certain materials carry a surcharge because the landfill or recycler charges us for them. A mattress or box spring is +$25 each, a refrigerant appliance like a fridge, freezer, window unit or dehumidifier is +$40, tires are +$8 each, and paint and e-waste are billed at whatever the disposal facility charges. Stairs add $25 per flight above the first, which matters in the three-story townhomes and walk-up apartment buildings off Carolina Place Parkway and Park Road. A heavy or awkward item such as a treadmill, a large appliance or an oversized sectional starts at +$40. Pianos, gun safes, hot tubs and slate pool tables are quoted separately and never go at the single-item rate, and same-day service is +$40.

The typical Pineville job is a garage or attic cleanout in an established subdivision, a basement and shed purge on one of the older lots near Main Street, or a full apartment clear-out at turnover. We load it, sweep the spot, and separate metal, e-waste and anything donatable before it goes to the landfill.

Where can Pineville residents legally get rid of furniture, mattresses and appliances?

Inside Pineville town limits, trash and yard debris go out every Wednesday and recycling every other Wednesday, and bulky items have to be scheduled in advance with Waste Pro at 980-255-3800 rather than dragged to the curb and left. Anything that will not fit in the green roll-out cart needs advance notice, and contractor-generated debris is never the town's responsibility.

The town's hauler will not take construction debris, electronics including TVs and computers, tires, paint, batteries, automotive fluids, pool chemicals or fluorescent bulbs. Those go to a Mecklenburg County facility. The closest full-service option is the Foxhole Recycling Center at 17131 Lancaster Highway, open Monday through Saturday 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., with the landfill side open weekdays only. For smaller loads, the staffed William R. Davie center at 4635 Pineville-Matthews Road is a shorter drive from the east side of town. Some materials carry a county disposal fee, so call 980-314-3867 before you load your own truck.

One Pineville quirk catches people constantly: several subdivisions carry a Pineville mailing address but are not on the town's collection route, including Cardinal Woods, Danby, Woodside Falls, Winghurst and Bridlestone. If you live in one of those, the town bulky-pickup process does not apply to you at all, and a private haul is usually the only realistic option.

  • Bagged household trash and cut, stacked yard debris: Wednesday curbside inside town limits
  • Bulky furniture and large items: scheduled in advance with Waste Pro, not left at the curb
  • TVs, computers, tires, paint, batteries, pool chemicals, fluorescent tubes: county centers only
  • Renovation and contractor debris: not collected by the town at all

Do you charge a trip fee to come to Pineville?

No. Pineville is Zone 1, which means no trip fee. Our base is in Steele Creek, so Pineville is one of the closest towns we serve and there is no travel surcharge added to junk removal, TV mounting, labor or flooring here.

We are often available same day in Pineville, though we do not guarantee it. Same-day scheduling adds $40. Because Pineville is close, we can usually work a short job into an existing route without pushing anything else, which is harder to do for towns further out.

Practical access notes for this town: gated and back-of-house parking in some of the newer subdivisions means we may need a code or a gate held; the older streets around Main Street, Dover Street and Polk Street have narrow driveways and tight rear alleys, so a 6x10 utility trailer often beats the 16ft box there; and the retail corridor along Carolina Place Parkway, US 521 and NC 51 backs up badly on Saturday afternoons, so we schedule mall-adjacent apartment jobs earlier in the day when we can. If your HOA restricts trailer parking or move-out hours, tell us when you book and we will plan around it.

How much does TV mounting cost in Pineville?

TV mounting in Pineville is $99 for sets up to 60 inches, $149 for 61 to 75 inches, $199 for 76 to 85 inches, and 86 inches and up is custom quoted with a two-person minimum. Mounting over a fireplace or into stone or brick starts at $199. A second or third TV mounted on the same visit gets 25% off the mount labor.

Add-ons are never discounted: stick-on cord concealment is $39, in-wall cord routing starts at $89, a flat mount is $40 and a full-motion mount is $65. Prices assume you already have a bracket unless you buy a mount from us.

In Pineville the two common jobs look very different. The newer subdivisions built around the I-485 corridor tend to have a stone or brick fireplace in the great room, which is the from-$199 category and often needs masonry anchors plus in-wall routing to get the cables down clean. Renters in the apartment communities near Carolina Place usually want a straightforward flat mount on a stud wall with stick-on concealment, since drilling into drywall for in-wall routing is often prohibited by the lease. In the older homes near Main Street, plaster, skip-sheathed walls and off-standard stud spacing are normal, so we probe before we drill.

Can you help with moving, loading and handyman work in Pineville?

Yes. Hourly labor in Pineville is $70/hr for a solo handyman with a 2-hour minimum, or $135/hr for a two-person crew with a 2-hour minimum. Add a utility trailer for +$35/hr or the 16ft enclosed trailer for +$60/hr, so a two-person crew with the 16ft box is $195/hr. After the minimum we bill in 30-minute increments, not rounded up to the hour.

On Saturday and Sunday the two-person hourly crew carries a 3-hour minimum. The solo handyman stays at the 2-hour minimum on weekends. Flat-rate work like TV mounting and junk removal has no minimum at all. A 15% Sunday uplift applies only to flat-rate work and never to hourly crew work.

This covers loading and unloading a rental truck or PODS container, in-home moves like getting a sleeper sofa up to a bonus room, storage-unit runs, furniture assembly, shelving and TV consoles, door and hardware fixes, gutter and light fixture work, and general punch-list items before a lease turnover or listing photos. Pineville generates a lot of partial moves, one room, one garage, one storage unit, which is exactly what hourly labor is for.

What does new flooring cost in a Pineville home?

Flooring installation is $2.50 per square foot for LVP and laminate and $3.50 per square foot for herringbone and premium patterns. Tear-out and haul-away of the old floor is $1.00 per square foot, and subfloor prep is $70/hr. There is a minimum of 200 square feet or $500, whichever is greater.

Pineville splits cleanly into two kinds of flooring job. The homes around the Main Street historic core and the old Dover mill area often have multiple layers stacked over decades, squeaky or uneven subfloor, and out-of-square rooms, so budget for subfloor prep hours and expect the tear-out to reveal something. The newer subdivisions built out along I-485 and US 521 are the opposite: builder-grade carpet over flat, level subfloor in an open main level, which is fast, predictable square footage.

The tear-out line matters here more than in most towns, because Pineville's residential collection will not take renovation debris and old carpet and pad are exactly that. Rolling the haul into the flooring job at $1.00 per square foot means the pile never sits at your curb waiting for a pickup that is not coming.
